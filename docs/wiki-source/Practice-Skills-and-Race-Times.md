# 🏁 Practice Skills and Race Times

## Progress through skills in order

| Level | Focus | Move on when… |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — Input check | Correct axes and throttle minimum in Liftoff | Every channel moves the expected way |
| 1 — Basic control | Smooth throttle and gentle turns | You can make deliberate inputs without fighting the sticks |
| 2 — Orientation | Turn left/right, fly away/towards, regain orientation | You can recover calmly after turning around |
| 3 — Lines | Wide circuits and figure-eights | You can repeat a line rather than improvise every lap |
| 4 — Gates | Look ahead, small corrections, stable exits | More gates completed with fewer hard corrections |
| 5 — Time trial | Same track, same quad preset and rates | Personal-best attempts are repeatable, not one lucky lap |
| 6 — Freestyle flow | Link turns and manoeuvres smoothly | You can plan the next move without losing control |

Do not use a time trial as the first measure of progress. Clean lines, fewer crashes and better recoveries are meaningful improvements.

## 🎯 Useful practice challenges

- [ ] 5 minutes: roll and pitch input check, then one calm lap.
- [ ] 10 minutes: smooth left/right turns on a wide course.
- [ ] 15 minutes: repeat one figure-eight and identify one source of over-correction.
- [ ] 20 minutes: same circuit for three attempts; record clean gates and best time.
- [ ] 30+ minutes: two focused blocks separated by a pause; review one replay before continuing.
