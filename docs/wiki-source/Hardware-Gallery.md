## How to use these images

- [ ] Point out the frame, battery, motors, props, FC/ESC, camera and antennas on a real build photo.
- [ ] Compare an FC/ESC stack against an AIO board.
- [ ] Identify the video antenna / video receiver separately from the ELRS control receiver.
- [ ] Find the exact manufacturer manual before copying any connector, solder pad or motor-order detail.
- [ ] Save your own build photos with short captions and date; avoid showing personal addresses or identifying information in backgrounds.

Images are embedded from their original public sources to keep attribution and current product pages close to the visual. Product images may change, move, or be removed by their owners.
