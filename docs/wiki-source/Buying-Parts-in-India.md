## 🧪 Example of a version/compatibility trap

The [BETAFPV Cetus X product page](https://betafpv.com/products/cetus-x-brushless-quadcopter) documents different configurations: an ELRS 2.4 GHz / Betaflight-oriented version and a different FrSky/Cetus-FC version. This illustrates why you must choose the **exact option/SKU**, not simply a drone family name. The page's listed stock and options can change.

Likewise, a [Walksnail Avatar HD Pro kit](https://www.caddxfpv.com/products/walksnail-avatar-hd-pro-kit) has specific input-voltage, mounting, goggles and recording features. A different Avatar revision may not share every feature. Verify the exact kit/camera/VTX revision and matching goggles or receiver.

## ⚠️ What to verify before checkout

- [ ] **Receiver:** exact ExpressLRS frequency band (2.4 GHz vs sub-GHz), hardware target and compatible firmware/configuration.
- [ ] **Video ecosystem:** analog, Walksnail, DJI, or HDZero are not interchangeable by assumption. Verify air unit/VTX, receiver/goggles and antenna compatibility.
- [ ] **Frame and stack:** frame size, FC/ESC mount spacing, board outline/height and camera cage dimensions all fit.
- [ ] **Battery:** cell count and voltage range are supported by ESC, FC/regulators, video unit and motors; connector and physical dimensions fit.
- [ ] **Motors and props:** motor mounting, shaft/prop mount, KV, prop diameter/pitch/blade count, battery S-count and motor/ESC current support suit each other.
- [ ] **Antennas:** connector gender/type, polarization and frequency match the module; VTX cooling/antenna requirements are respected.
- [ ] **Software support:** exact board revision has a supported target and a recoverable firmware/backup path.
- [ ] **Total cost:** include goggles/display, charger, batteries, tools, spare props, motors/arms, soldering supplies, shipping, customs/import tax where relevant, and repair margin.
- [ ] Listing details, GST invoice, return/warranty policy, seller stock and expected delivery are understood.

## 💡 Beginner recommendation

Do not pick a complete build until you've decided **where it will fly** and whether your first aircraft should be a small indoor/outdoor micro or a larger freestyle quad. Your Pocket ELRS only describes the control transmitter; it does not determine goggles/video compatibility. Use a ready-to-fly (RTF) or bind-and-fly (BNF) kit only after verifying the actual version is 2.4 GHz ELRS and the video system meets your plan. For a custom build, fill the [First Build Checklist](First-Build-Checklist) BOM and resolve all open questions before purchasing.
