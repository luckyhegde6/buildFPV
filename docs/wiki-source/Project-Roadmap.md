# Project Roadmap

Use the following order to keep complexity proportional to your experience.

## Stage 1 — Radio and simulator

**Learn:** stick axes, mode/rates, USB input, calibration, and simulator recovery habits.

**Exit evidence:** reliable controller setup, controlled takeoff/landing, broad circuits and figure-eights, plus dated practice notes.

## Stage 2 — System understanding

**Learn:** control link versus video link, FC/ESC/motors, power path, arming, failsafe and component compatibility.

**Exit evidence:** explain the system without confusing RC transmitter and video transmitter; know what each firmware configures.

## Stage 3 — Plan a simple aircraft

**Learn:** frame fit, battery voltage, motor/prop matching, ESC current headroom, receiver protocol and video ecosystem.

**Exit evidence:** fully sourced bill of materials, completed compatibility checklist, budget, spares plan and experienced review.

## Stage 4 — Safe setup

**Learn:** wiring diagrams, solder quality, correct firmware target, receiver mapping, motor order, arming and failsafe.

**Exit evidence:** all propeller-off checks complete and configuration backed up.

## Stage 5 — First flights and maintenance

**Learn:** site choice and local rules, preflight, conservative flying, battery care, inspection and repair records.

**Exit evidence:** repeatable flight-day routine, and no unresolved checks before flight.

## Stage 6 — Explore reference projects when there is a clear reason

- **ExpressLRS:** relevant immediately because the Pocket ELRS uses the RC-link ecosystem. Start with official documentation; understand bind, link quality, packet rate and failsafe.
- **TBS Source One:** useful as a frame design/build reference during aircraft planning. A frame is not a complete electronics BOM; verify revision, dimensions and hardware.
- **Gyroflow:** later, after recording footage and wanting post-production stabilization. Not required to fly.
- **OpenVTx:** later, only after identifying the exact supported VTX hardware and a recovery method.
- **OpenHD:** advanced experimentation with an open digital video/telemetry link; adds hardware/software/network/RF complexity and is not needed for basic flying.
- **RubyFPV:** explore after flight-controller concepts are familiar. Verify current project status and exact board support before selecting firmware.
- **awesome-flying-fpv:** discovery index; validate each destination with current official docs.

## Engineering habits

Maintain decision records: question, options, official sources/date, assumptions, chosen approach, trade-offs and evidence. Revisit decisions when a specification or project status changes.
