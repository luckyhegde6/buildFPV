# 🎮 Daily Liftoff Routine — small sessions count

**Target:** build a durable habit, not chase the longest session. Choose the smallest duration you can honestly complete today. It is okay to choose 5 minutes.

New to FPV? First complete the [mandatory beginner FPV playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLwoDb7WF6c8lCKhQOTy-Vb9LfW0VAIrTP) in sequence while practising in [Liftoff FPV Drone Racing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/410340/Liftoff_FPV_Drone_Racing/) or [Liftoff: Micro Drones](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1432320/Liftoff_Micro_Drones/). This is project onboarding, not formal certification.

## 2. Session recipes

| Time | A focused routine |
|---|---|
| **5 min** | 1 min axis check + 3 min one drill + 1 min reflection |
| **10 min** | 2 min warm-up + 6 min one drill + 2 min log |
| **15 min** | 3 min warm-up + 9 min drill + 3 min log/replay review |
| **20 min** | 3 min warm-up + 12 min drill + 3 min time trial/review + 2 min log |
| **30+ min** | 5 min warm-up + 2 × 10 min focused blocks with a short break + 5 min review/log |

These are habit templates, not a prescription. Stop if tired/frustrated; reduce difficulty rather than practising sloppy inputs for volume.

## 3. Pick one drill

- [ ] Stick-axis isolation: roll only, pitch only, yaw only, throttle only
- [ ] Smooth throttle: avoid sudden altitude changes
- [ ] Wide turns both directions: keep the horizon/heading mentally tracked
- [ ] Figure-eight: consistent radius and no frantic corrections
- [ ] Gates: look ahead, make small corrections, avoid chasing the frame
- [ ] Orientation recovery: deliberately turn around and regain orientation calmly
- [ ] Landing: approach at controlled speed, settle, disarm
- [ ] Race/time trial: record track, quad preset and best lap

## 5. Use the radio you have

Pocket ELRS is enough to start. If you later use a Boxer, TX1, TX16S, or another transmitter, log the actual model and verify its USB/controller behavior separately. The same stick-control objectives transfer, but USB modes, firmware and radio menus may differ.

### Make it frictionless

- Keep the radio, USB data cable, and simulator ready in one place.
- Use a recurring calendar reminder if it helps—but do not let missing a day break the habit.
- Make a GitHub issue from the repository's **Daily Liftoff Session** template, or copy `docs/learning-log/templates/daily-session.md`.
- Review your logs weekly. Repeat the most useful drill; do not change rates every session just to feel busy.
