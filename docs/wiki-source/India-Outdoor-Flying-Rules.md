# 🇮🇳 India — outdoor flying and radio-compliance checks

This is a **verify-before-flight checklist**, not legal advice. FPV rules can depend on aircraft category/weight, operation, location, equipment, and changes in official rules or airspace zones. A hobby label does not make every flight or radio setting automatically permitted.

## Before flying outdoors

- [ ] Check current official Ministry of Civil Aviation / DGCA guidance and applicable amendments.
- [ ] Check the current Digital Sky airspace map for the actual flight location on the day of flight; zone status can change.
- [ ] Confirm requirements that apply to the specific aircraft, operation and category, including registration/identification or prior permission if applicable.
- [ ] Check radio equipment/band/frequency/power requirements with the appropriate official authorities and device documentation.
- [ ] Confirm site permission and keep clear of people, roads, airports, restricted sites, and other aircraft.
- [ ] Verify safe visual awareness/spotter arrangements and the applicable operating restrictions.
- [ ] Do not rely on a forum post, old video, seller listing, or a previous flight as proof that a location or setup is legal today.

## Official starting points

- [Ministry of Civil Aviation — Drone Rules, 2021](https://www.civilaviation.gov.in/ministry-documents/rules/drones-rules-2021-dated-25-august-2021)
- [Digital Sky](https://digitalsky.dgca.gov.in/)
- [DGCA](https://www.dgca.gov.in/)
- [Department of Telecommunications — Wireless Planning & Coordination](https://dot.gov.in/wireless-planning-coordination)

**Important:** The Drone Rules, 2021 page is the original rules source; it is not a guarantee that no subsequent amendment, circular, airspace update, or other requirement applies. Check the latest official material on the date of operation and ask the relevant authority when the situation is unclear.
